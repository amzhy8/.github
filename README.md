## 🇬🇧 English Version

### 🚀 Who We Are
Welcome to the official open-source community of **GEEKBMS** (Chengdu Geeknest Technology Co., Ltd.). We are a forward-thinking technology company specializing in the research, design, and development of advanced **Battery Management Systems (BMS)**.

### 🎯 Our Mission: Hardware Innovation Through Open Source
The traditional pure hardware BMS market has fallen into a cycle of hyper-competition, shrinking margins, and declining hardware quality. At GEEKBMS, we choose a different path: **Long-Termism**. 

We are committed to making our production-proven, high-quality hardware designs **100% open-source and free**. By lowering the barriers to development and prototyping, we empower global independent engineers, makers, and SMEs to build safer, more reliable power solutions without the headache of low-quality BMS designs.

### 🚀 关于极客电巢
欢迎来到 **GEEKBMS（成都极客电巢科技有限公司）** 官方开源社区。我们是一家专注于先进**电池管理系统（BMS）**研发与设计的硬核科技企业。

### 🎯 我们的使命：开源驱动硬件创新
当前的纯硬件BMS保护板市场正陷入恶性低价内卷与产品质量下滑的泥潭。极客电巢选择了一条截然不同的道路：**坚守长期主义**。

我们致力于将公司经过量产验证的高质量纯硬件保护板方案**完全、无保留地开源**。通过降低开发、选型与打样的门槛，我们旨在助力全球独立工程师与中小企业快速搭建高效、安全的电源系统，彻底告别劣质BMS。
